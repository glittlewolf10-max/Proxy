# Study preferences

I'm studying for Spanish class. I have a bad memory, so build every study tool around the method below. It's my main method.

## My study method

1. Split the list into **groups of 5**, in the same order as my class sheet. If the last group would have only 1 item, fold it into the group before it.
2. **Study one item at a time.** Show the prompt (picture, map location or English) together with the Spanish answer, and have me type it once before the next one appears. Don't show the whole group at once.
3. **Quiz one item at a time from memory.** Show only that one item. On a map, highlight only that one country and gray out everything else, with no other numbers or names.
4. **When I miss, show the answer and make me type it in** before I can move on.
5. **If a pass through the group had any mistake, repeat the whole group** in mixed order until I get one pass with no mistakes. Only then move on to the next 5.
6. After the last group, do a round with **everything together** (same rules), then offer a **full test**.

## Other preferences

- Typed answers only (data entry), never multiple choice.
- A word passes when **at least 75% of its letters are right** (edit distance ÷ answer length). Accents and ñ count by default, with a setting to turn that off. Ignore capital letters and punctuation like ¿ ? ¡ !.
- Accept every correct form, like *Llueve* or *Está lloviendo*, and *Me llamo* or *Mi nombre es*.
- Buttons and instructions in **English**; the vocabulary stays in Spanish.
- Always double-check my class sheets and tell me what's wrong: wrong words, missing accents, wrong English.
- Modern design with motion. It has to work on a phone and a Chromebook.
